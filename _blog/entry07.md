
# Entry 7: Presentation and elevator pitch
##### 6/7/26

### Presentation
During class my classmates and I had to go up to the front and each one of us would present their project to the whole class. While students were presenting we had to pay attention and ask questions or make comments about their project or presantation.

When it was my turn to present, I talked about my topic for my project, my tool, and the process for making my website along with its contend and my takeaways.

### Elevator Pitch
After completing our projects, we each had to go to the cafeteria in order to give a show our project to other students, and the judges who would choose a winner. Through this showcase we had to give a short "elevator pitch" about our project, explaining the important parts while keeping it brief.

### Takeaways
During this project my communication and listening skills improved because I had to not only present my website to other classmates in a professional manner, but I also had to be respectful and listen while they presented theirs, this while also asking questions about their website.

* I had to present to other people which helped me be more clear and concise when trying to communicate my ideas
* I had to listen to other people's presentations without getting distracted and while asking them questions.
